# 🅲 C Tier — The Land of “Meh”

> _“Nothing is broken.  
> Nothing is memorable.”_

---

## 📌 What Is C Tier?

**C Tier** is the realm of **functional mediocrity**.

These games:
- Work as intended
- Rarely frustrate
- Rarely excite

> C Tier isn’t bad —  
> it’s **forgettable**.

---

## 🧠 The Role of C Tier

C Tier exists so higher tiers matter.

This is where:
- Safe design lives
- Committees win
- Risk quietly disappears

These games fill shelves, libraries, and backlogs —  
then vanish from memory.

---

## 🚫 What C Tier Is NOT

C Tier is not:
- Broken
- Offensive
- Unplayable

It’s worse than that.

It’s **uninspiring**.

---

## ✅ Common C-Tier Traits

---

### 🎮 1. Serviceable Gameplay

- Controls are fine
- Mechanics function
- Systems exist

Nothing invites mastery.  
Nothing resists you.

---

### 🎨 2. Generic Presentation

- Passable visuals
- Forgettable music
- Safe art direction

You won’t hate looking at it —  
you just won’t remember it.

---

### 🧩 3. Design by Template

- Familiar structures
- Predictable pacing
- Minimal experimentation

You’ve played this game before —  
just with a different logo.

---

### 🔁 4. Minimal Replay Value

Once is enough.

You finish it and think:
> “Yeah… that was a game.”

Then you uninstall.

---

## ⚠️ Typical C-Tier Limitations

| Issue | Impact |
|----|------|
| No identity | No lasting impression |
| Overly safe design | No risk, no reward |
| Flat pacing | Emotional neutrality |
| Forgettable systems | No discussion value |

Nothing hurts —  
nothing heals either.

## 🧠 How You Recognize a C-Tier Game

You say things like:
- “It was okay”
- “Not bad”
- “I don’t really remember much”
- “I dropped it halfway, not sure why”

No strong emotions —  
positive or negative.

---

## 🏁 Final Definition

> **C Tier is where games exist without impact.**

They don’t offend.  
They don’t inspire.

They simply **pass through you**.

---

## ⭐ TL;DR

C-Tier games:
- Are competent
- Are safe
- Are forgettable

They’re not mistakes —  
they’re **missed opportunities**.
