# 🟥 F Tier — Design Failure

> _“This should not have shipped.”_

---

## 📌 What Is F Tier?

**F Tier** is not about taste.  
It is not about difficulty.  
It is not about preference.

> **F Tier is about fundamental failure.**

These games do not merely disappoint —  
they **malfunction as games**.

---

## 🧠 The Role of F Tier

F Tier exists for accountability.

This is where:
- Quality control failed
- Design principles were ignored
- The player experience was sacrificed

These titles become **case studies**, not experiences.

---

## 🚫 What F Tier Is NOT

F Tier is not:
- Low-budget indie experiments
- Intentional joke games
- Dated classics showing their age

F Tier games **had no excuse**.

---

## ❌ Absolute Failures That Define F Tier

---

### 💥 1. Non-Functional Core Systems

- Controls don’t respond
- Mechanics don’t work
- Core loop is broken

You are not challenged —  
you are obstructed.

---

### 🧠 2. No Design Intent

- Systems feel random
- Nothing is explained
- Nothing connects

This is not chaos by design.  
This is **absence of design**.

---

### ⏳ 3. Disrespect for Player Time

- Forced repetition
- Unskippable padding
- Meaningless progression

The game consumes time without giving value.

---

### 🧩 4. Technical Failure

- Game-breaking bugs
- Crashes
- Corrupt saves
- Inconsistent performance

At this point, critique becomes irrelevant.
