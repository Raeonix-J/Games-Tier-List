# 🟨 E Tier — Fundamentally Flawed

> _“Good intentions. Broken foundations.”_

---

## 📌 What Is E Tier?

**E Tier** is where games fail at a **core level**.

These games:
- Have ideas
- Have effort
- Sometimes even have style

But something **essential** doesn’t work.

> E Tier games are not bad by accident —  
> they are broken by design or execution.

---

## 🧠 The Role of E Tier

E Tier exists as a warning.

This is where:
- Systems fight each other
- Mechanics undermine the experience
- Vision collapses under reality

These games teach studios what **not** to do.

---

## 🚫 What E Tier Is NOT

E Tier is not:
- Unfinished shovelware
- Meme games
- Asset flips

Those belong lower.

E Tier games **try** —  
and still fail.

---

## ❌ Core Failures of E Tier Games

---

### 🧱 1. Broken Core Loop

The main thing you do:
- Isn’t fun
- Isn’t satisfying
- Or actively frustrates

No amount of polish can fix this.

---

### ⚙️ 2. Conflicting Systems

- Mechanics cancel each other out
- Difficulty curves make no sense
- Player incentives clash

The game doesn’t know what it wants.

---

### 🎮 3. Player Punishment Without Purpose

- Cheap difficulty
- Unfair mechanics
- Time-wasting systems

Failure doesn’t teach.  
It just **annoys**.

---

### 🧩 4. Design That Ignores Feedback

The game:
- Refuses to adapt
- Doesn’t respect player time
- Feels hostile unintentionally

Challenge becomes resentment.

---

## ⚠️ Typical E-Tier Problems

| Issue | Result |
|----|-------|
| Poor controls | Constant frustration |
| Unclear goals | Player confusion |
| Bad pacing | Fatigue |
| Unbalanced systems | Forced playstyles |

The player is fighting the game —  
not the challenge.
