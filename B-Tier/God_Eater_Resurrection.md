# ⚔️ God Eater Resurrection

## Tier: **B**

> *A fast, stylish, and content-rich monster hunter that excels in story, customization, and co-op — but shows its PSP roots through repetition and AI limitations when played solo.*

## ✅ What Earns It the B

### 1. Combat Is Fast, Fluid, and Deep
The God Arc system — switching between melee, gun, and shield mid-combat — gives the game a kinetic energy that feels great. The weapon count is massive, the upgrade paths are robust, and combat rewards aggressive, adaptive play. There is a genuine skill ceiling here that keeps the loop engaging well past the early hours.

### 2. Story and World-Building Punch Well Above Its Weight
For a monster-hunting game, God Eater Resurrection has a surprisingly rich narrative. The post-apocalyptic setting, the lore around Aragami, and the cast of God Eaters give the game a distinct identity and a dark atmosphere that sets it apart from the genre. It excels in story, world-building, characters, and music in ways the genre rarely prioritises.

### 3. Customization Is Genuinely Impressive
Deep character creation, weapon crafting, upgrade trees, and loadout building give the game enormous depth for players who enjoy optimisation. If you enjoy min-maxing your gear and build, this game will consume you entirely.

### 4. Content Volume Is Enormous
Completing the story alone takes upwards of 40 hours. For players who pursue every side mission available, the total runtime pushes toward 100 hours. For a single purchase, that is exceptional value.

### 5. Co-op Is Where the Game Truly Shines
Online co-op with up to 3 other players transforms the experience into something genuinely special — coordinating hunts, sharing resources, and tackling Aragami together is the version of this game the developers clearly intended. When played this way, it earns its reputation as one of the best alternatives in the monster-hunting genre.

---

## ❌ Why It Doesn't Go Higher

### 1. Solo Play Is a Significant Step Down
Without co-op partners, the game devolves into a noticeably more repetitive and less effective experience — one player, a massive enemy with an enormous health pool, and AI companions that can revive you but little else. The game was designed around co-op and it shows painfully when you go it alone.

### 2. Mission Structure Is Repetitive by Design
Every mission follows the same loop: deploy, hunt target Aragami, return to base, collect rewards, repeat. There is no ability to chain missions back-to-back without returning to base between each one — a time-consuming friction point that makes the repetition feel worse than it needs to be.

### 3. It Shows Its PSP Age
God Eater Resurrection is a remaster of a PSP game from 2010, and that heritage is visible. The mission design, arena layouts, and enemy behaviour patterns have all been outpaced by games released since. By 2016 standards, it felt dated in practice — and that gap has only widened since.

### 4. Enemy AI and Camera Have Real Weaknesses
The camera can become awkward during close-quarters fights with large Aragami, and the enemy AI — while serviceable — lacks the sophistication that the genre had developed elsewhere by the time of this release.

## 🏁 Final Verdict

God Eater Resurrection is a game that rewards the right kind of player enormously and frustrates the wrong kind just as much. Its story, customisation, music, and co-op experience are all genuinely excellent — enough to place individual pillars at A tier. But the heavy repetition, the steep solo difficulty curve, and the visible age of its PSP foundations keep the overall package firmly at **B Tier**. It is one of the best entries in the monster-hunting genre outside of its most famous competitor — but it needs the right conditions to truly shine.

> **Who should play it:** Monster-hunting fans, anime RPG lovers, co-op players, customisation enthusiasts, and anyone looking for a story-driven alternative in the genre.
>
> **Who should skip it:** Solo-only players, anyone with low tolerance for mission repetition, or players expecting modern enemy AI and level design.
